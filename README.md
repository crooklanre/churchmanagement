# Church Management

> Give your church team more room to care for people.

## The Opportunity

Church Management brings everyday church operations into one coordinated experience. Members can stay connected to their activities, while church teams can organize follow-up, attendance, volunteer coordination, and community updates.

## Who It Serves

Church administrators, ministry teams, volunteers, and members.

## What It Makes Possible

- Help church teams keep member information and community activity organized
- Support attendance, volunteer coordination, and follow-up
- Keep members informed about activities and important updates
- Give different participants a view suited to their role

## How People Use It

1. Members use the service to stay informed and connected to their church activities.
2. Volunteers and workers use it to coordinate attendance, events, and follow-up.
3. Church administrators use it to organize community operations and keep the congregation supported.

## How It Connects

- A ConnectWBT ecosystem product focused on the practical needs of churches and religious communities.
- Complements ConnectWBT’s broader goal of helping separate services work together around the people and communities they serve.
